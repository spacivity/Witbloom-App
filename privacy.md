## At a glance

You do not need a WitBloom account. Your player profile, detailed scores and practice insights are stored on your device, not uploaded to a WitBloom server. Optional usage analytics is off by default and is available only to profiles with an age of 18 or older. Apple handles purchases. Declining analytics does not restrict gameplay or Premium access.

## Information stored on your device

The app stores:

- Your name or nickname, age, pronouns and optional sex selection, to personalise the experience. You can use a nickname. Age helps select starting difficulty; sex is not used to determine difficulty or assess ability.
- Game scores, play dates, game settings and detailed performance measurements, such as accuracy, moves and reaction times, to display personal bests, activity and practice insights.
- Sound preferences, daily and weekly reminder preferences, reminder times and local notification identifiers.
- The weekly free-game rotation and your analytics consent choice.

These values are held in local app storage. WitBloom has no account-based profile or score synchronisation. Your operating system's backup or device-transfer features may include app data, depending on your settings; those services are controlled separately by Apple.

Practice insights are calculated on the device. They are observations about game performance, not medical assessments, diagnoses or decisions about your health.

## Optional usage analytics

If you opt in, we use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand which features people use and improve the app. Our custom events report screen names, game starts and completions, visits to the Premium screen, purchase-plan selections, restore-button use and daily-reminder enable/disable actions. Selecting a purchase plan is not itself a confirmed purchase.

Our custom events do not include your name, age, sex, pronouns, scores, reaction measurements or practice insights. We do not assign a custom account/user ID.

After opt-in, the Firebase SDK may also process an app-instance identifier, app and device information, session activity, purchase/subscription metadata and an approximate region derived from your IP address. This is pseudonymous information, not anonymous information. It does not require access to precise device location. See [Google's Analytics data-disclosure explanation](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/10285841?hl=en).

The app disables advertising consent, IDFA support and IDFV collection. It does not use analytics to deliver targeted advertising or track you across other companies' apps and websites. We do not sell your personal information.

You can change your choice in **Settings → Privacy & analytics → Help improve WitBloom**. Turning it off stops future analytics collection and resets the local analytics identity. It does not automatically delete information already received by Google. Analytics events may be buffered while offline after you opt in. See [Firebase's collection controls](https://firebase.google.com/docs/analytics/ios/configure-data-collection).

## Purchases and subscriptions

Apple processes monthly and annual subscriptions and the one-off lifetime purchase through the App Store. The app reads verified StoreKit purchase and entitlement information to unlock Premium and restore access. We do not receive your payment-card details through the app or send purchase receipts to a WitBloom backend.

Apple processes purchase information under its own [Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). If you enable analytics, Firebase may separately measure purchase events as explained above. Deleting the app does not cancel an Apple subscription; manage subscriptions through your Apple account.

## Reminders

Daily practice and weekly game-unlock reminders are optional local notifications scheduled on your device with your permission. The current app does not register a remote push token or send your reminder schedule to a WitBloom notification server. You can disable reminders in the app or in iOS notification settings.

Local profile, score and preference data remain in the app until removed. You can edit your profile in Settings. The current version has no in-app erase-all control: use iOS **Delete App**, not **Offload App**, to remove the app's local data. Backups may retain copies under your device/backup settings. We cannot remotely access or erase your local scores.

## Younger players

Analytics is unavailable when the profile age is under 18 or unknown. Changing a profile to an age under 18 revokes the app's saved analytics consent. Age is entered by the player and checked locally; it is not identity or age verification. Local game personalisation still works without analytics.

A parent or guardian can contact us with questions or concerns. Please do not send a child's personal details unless necessary to explain the concern.

## Your choices and rights

Depending on your location and the circumstances, you may have rights to access, correct or erase personal information we hold, restrict or object to processing, receive a portable copy, and withdraw consent. Contact [support+witbloom@spacivity.com](mailto:support+witbloom@spacivity.com) to make a request. We may need proportionate information to verify it; these rights are subject to applicable limits.

You may also complain to your local data-protection authority. In the UK, this is the [Information Commissioner's Office](https://ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint/).
