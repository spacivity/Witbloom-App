# WitBloom Support

WitBloom is an offline-first collection of short recreational cognitive games for iPhone and iPad. WitBloom is part of **Spacivity Ltd trading as Spacivity Games**. This page explains how to solve common problems and contact us.

## Contact us

Email [support+witbloom@spacivity.com](mailto:support+witbloom@spacivity.com) for help with WitBloom.

To help us investigate, include:

- A short description of what happened and what you expected.
- The steps that led to the problem.
- Your iPhone or iPad model, iOS version and WitBloom app version.
- A screenshot or screen recording, if useful.
- Whether restarting the app or device changed the problem.

Please do not email passwords, payment-card details, Apple Account credentials, medical information or other sensitive personal information. WitBloom has no user account, so we will never ask for your password. Do not send an App Store receipt unless support specifically requests information from it; redact unrelated details first.

## Quick troubleshooting

For most issues:

1. Fully close and reopen WitBloom.
2. Check that you have the latest WitBloom version from the App Store and the latest compatible iOS update.
3. Restart your device.
4. If the issue needs internet access—such as loading App Store prices, restoring a purchase or opening a research link—check your connection and try again.

Most games, scores, insights, sounds and reminders work locally. A network connection is required for App Store purchase services, external research pages and optional analytics delivery.

## Premium purchases

Apple processes all WitBloom payments. WitBloom does not receive your payment-card details.

If Premium is missing after a purchase:

1. Confirm that the device is signed in to the same Apple Account used for the purchase.
2. Open **Settings → Premium & Subscriptions** in WitBloom.
3. Tap **Restore Purchases** and allow the App Store check to finish.
4. Restart WitBloom if access does not update immediately.

Restoring does not charge you again. If it still fails, email us with the product type—monthly, annual or lifetime—and the approximate purchase date. Do not send payment-card details.

To change or cancel a monthly or annual subscription, use **Manage Subscription** on WitBloom's Premium screen, or go to iOS **Settings → your name → Subscriptions → WitBloom**. Cancelling stops future renewal; it does not normally remove access before the current paid period ends. The lifetime purchase does not renew.

Refund eligibility and decisions are handled by Apple. Use [Apple's billing and refund support](https://support.apple.com/billing) for charge, refund or payment-method questions. See Apple's guidance for [restoring in-app purchases](https://support.apple.com/108096) and [cancelling subscriptions](https://support.apple.com/118428).

## Scores and offline data

Your profile, detailed game results, settings and insights are stored locally on your device. WitBloom does not provide an account or cloud score synchronisation. Scores may therefore differ between devices and support cannot remotely recover them.

Deleting WitBloom with iOS **Delete App** removes its local app data. **Offload App** may preserve that data. Reinstalling or deleting the app may result in lost scores and settings, so use this only after considering that consequence. Your Apple purchase remains associated with your Apple Account and can be restored separately.

## Reminders are not appearing

- In WitBloom, open **Settings** and confirm the daily or weekly reminder is enabled.
- In iOS, open **Settings → Notifications → WitBloom** and allow notifications and sounds.
- Confirm that Focus modes or Scheduled Summary are not delaying the notification.
- Toggle the reminder off and on again to reschedule it.

WitBloom currently schedules reminders locally on your device; it does not require a WitBloom account or remote push-messaging service.

## Sound problems

- Check WitBloom's sound and soundscape controls in **Settings**.
- Raise the device media volume and check Silent Mode, Focus settings and connected Bluetooth audio devices.
- Reopen the app if another audio app or an interruption has changed the audio session.

Include the affected game and sound name when reporting audio that is too loud, quiet, delayed or overlapping incorrectly.

## Optional analytics and privacy

Usage analytics is optional, off by default and only offered to adult profiles. You can change it under **Settings → Privacy & analytics**. Turning it off stops future collection and resets the local Firebase analytics identity; it does not delete events already received by Google.

For privacy questions or rights requests, email [support+witbloom@spacivity.com](mailto:support+witbloom@spacivity.com). Read the [WitBloom Privacy Policy](https://www.spacivity.com/games/witbloom/privacy). The repository's policy draft is also available in [PRIVACY.md](PRIVACY.md).

## Health and safety

WitBloom is recreational skills practice. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat or prevent a health condition. Scores and progress insights should not be treated as a measure of intelligence or a medical assessment. Contact a qualified healthcare professional for health concerns.

Take a break if play causes discomfort, eye strain, dizziness or frustration. Device accessibility and display settings may help make play more comfortable.

## Research links and content feedback

Each game includes an information page describing the task and research context. External research links require an internet connection and may be changed or removed by their publishers. Send reports of broken links, accessibility issues, incorrect wording or gameplay defects to our support email with the game name.
